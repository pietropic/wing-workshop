Directions

The workshop will be held at the Mathematics department of the University of Gothenburg and Chalmers University of Technology. The exact address of the main entrance is Chalmers Tvärgata 3, 412 58 Göteborg. You can see below an image of the entrance during summer.

Getting to Gothenburg

The closest airport is Göteborg Landvetter (GOT), about 25 km east of the city. Airport buses run from Landvetter to the city centre and stop at Korsvägen, which is a one tram stop from the department. Gothenburg Central Station has direct trains from Stockholm, Malmö and Copenhagen Airport.

Getting to the department

The nearest tram stops are Chalmers and Kapellplatsen, both a few minutes on foot from Chalmers Tvärgata 3. Tickets and timetables for trams and buses are available from Västtrafik; tickets cam be bought with contactless card on board. From Gothenburg Central Station the trip takes about 20 minutes.

Accommodation

Participants book their own accommodation. Hotels near Korsvägen, Avenyn or Linnéstaden are all within walking distance or a short tram ride of the department.
