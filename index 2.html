Winter Geometry Workshop in Gothenburg

The Winter Geometry Workshop is a small meeting in complex geometry, held from December 9 to December 11, 2026 at the Department of Mathematical Sciences of the University of Gothenburg and Chalmers University of Technology. The talks will range across complex geometry, from pluripotential theory to canonical metrics and K-stability, with links to mathematical physics.

There will be around ten talks over the three days, each 45 minutes, with plenty of time for discussion in between. Everyone is welcome to attend. Registration is free and closes on November 9th, 2026.
